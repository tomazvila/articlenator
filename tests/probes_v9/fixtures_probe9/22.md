# Three Months From Tuck Planche To Straddle Planche Is A Realistic Good Result

Achieving the straddle planche from a ten-second tuck planche hold in three months is a realistic and good result, even though some programs claim a six-week timeline.

## Why This Matters

Unrealistic expectations cause trainees to feel like failures when they do not hit aggressive timelines, reinforcing the [[Limited Beliefs Are The Primary Barrier To Calisthenics Skill Acquisition|limited beliefs]] that keep them stuck. Normalizing a three-month target keeps trainees motivated and consistent, preventing the abandonment of the skill when progress is slower than advertised.

## Details

- The six-week timeline is achievable for trainees with exceptional strength, recovery capacity, and the ability to train twice daily, but it is not the norm.
- Three months from tuck to straddle planche is a strong result that most trainees would be happy to achieve.
- Trainees who have been working on the planche for years and are still at the advanced tuck stage can realistically reach the straddle in one to two months with the correct progression system.
- The two bottleneck transitions (half-tuck to advanced tuck and half-pike to straddle) are the primary reason the timeline extends from six weeks to three months.
- The three-month timeline has been validated through testing on over 600 students over two years, producing consistent results that confirm it as realistic — the [[The Planche Progression System Is Effective For Trainees In Their Twenties Through Forties|planche progression system]] works across age groups and experience levels.

## Grounded Example

A trainee who has been stuck on the advanced tuck planche for eight months starts the six-week system. The half-tuck to advanced tuck transition takes three weeks, and the half-pike to straddle transition takes two weeks. After eleven weeks total, they achieve a three-second straddle planche hold. They consider this a success because they finally broke through the plateau that had stalled them for nearly a year.
